## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am building my career in **cybersecurity**, with a strong focus on **Security Operations and SOC Analyst roles**.

My learning journey is centered around developing practical skills in **SIEM, Splunk, SPL, log analysis, security monitoring, alert triage, threat detection, and security investigations**. I enjoy learning by building hands-on projects, practicing with real-world security datasets, and documenting my investigation and analysis workflows.

I am also developing a **cybersecurity homelab** to strengthen my understanding of security monitoring, Linux, networking, SIEM concepts, and SOC workflows.

Alongside my SOC-focused learning, I am interested in exploring how **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be applied to cybersecurity**.

My goal is to begin my professional cybersecurity career as a **SOC Analyst or Cybersecurity Analyst** and continue developing toward areas such as **Threat Detection, Incident Response, Threat Hunting, Security Engineering, and AI/ML-driven cybersecurity solutions**.
